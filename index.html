Our story

Started with a love of good coffee, old stories & quirky locals.

Haggard began in Brooksville, Florida. Approachable, fair-trade coffee rooted in the Nature Coast, served by a small-town crew that never quite learned to be normal.

Come for the roast, stay for the breakfast sammies, and leave with a story. Or a t-shirt. Probably both.
